# Throwing Hands (WIP)

Play chords with two hands in front of the laptop camera. Sound comes from the laptop speakers.

- **Left hand** — 1–5 fingers pick circles 1–5. Pinch plays circle 6.
- **Right hand** — shapes the chord while it plays: raise your hand for louder, lower it for quieter, tilt for brighter or darker tone, stick your thumb out to jump up an octave.
- **Circles** — click one, or press **space** / **→** for the next, and type a chord. **Enter** hides empty circles.

```bash
./run.sh
```

**Q** quits.

To capture sound with the usual Mac screen recorder: **Cmd+Shift+5** → pick a **Record** tool → **Options** → **Microphone** → **BlackHole 2ch**. That mix is the chords plus your singing from the laptop mic. Allow Microphone access when asked. You still hear the chords on the speakers.
